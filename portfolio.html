04 / how I work

A small loop,
honestly run.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A 30-minute call. I take notes in plain text. You don’t get a deck afterwards; you get a one-page brief I’ve written back to you.

  2. 02

    Make — small

    I send rough work early. We argue about it. We throw most of it away. By week two we usually know what the thing is.

  3. 03

    Make — final

    I ship in a branch you can review. You leave comments in the file, not in a doc. I close the loop in days, not weeks.

  4. 04

    Stay close

    I keep a quiet eye on the things I’ve built. Most of my clients email me a year later about something small; I usually say yes.

14 march 2025 · issue 19

A small site is a long letter.

Most of the sites I like best were made by one person who had something to say. Not a lot — just one thing, said slowly, and said twice. The medium of a personal site is a letter, not a billboard. The web has billboards now. We don’t need more.

02 february 2025 · issue 18

On the second visit.

I judge a site by its second visit. The first visit is novelty — the colours, the animation, the surprise. The second is where the design either earns its place or annoys you. Load time, line length, the weight of a button — these are the things that matter on the second visit.
